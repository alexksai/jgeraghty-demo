OUR HISTORY

After working in the Multi-Utility industry for many years, Jonjo Geraghty took his vast experience and reliable reputation and founded J Geraghty Ltd in 2014.

Today J Geraghty Utility Services is one of the leading Multi Utility Contractors in the UK, working with leading commercial organisations and domestic clients around the country, managing projects from start to finish on the infrastructure and connections of Water, Gas, Electricity and BT Fibre.
