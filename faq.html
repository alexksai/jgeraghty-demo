Frequently Asked Questions

Are you fully accredited?

Yes! We have all the relevant accreditations from GIRS, NERS and WIRS.

Once a project is accepted, we will assign you with a project manager who will fully advise you.

Yes! We work with domestic individuals up to large commercial outfits.

What are multi utility connections?

The supply of two or more utilities such as Water, Gas, Electricity and Fibre to a single or multiple end-points.

Where are you able to work in the UK?

Based in Halifax, West Yorkshire we predominantly work in Greater Manchester and Yorkshire, but also around the UK including Wales, Ireland and Scotland.

What services do you provide?

Installation connections of Multi Utilities to existing and new properties including Water, Gas, Electric and BT Fibre.
